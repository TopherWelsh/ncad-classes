🎤 The Scripting Rule

Your script should be integrated into your storyboards. This includes VO (Spoken Voiceover) and On-Screen (Words animated via Kinetic Type).

⚠ Pro Tip: Read it Out Loud!

Actually time yourself speaking the script. If your script takes 2 minutes to say, but your video is 60 seconds, you have a problem.

Dig Deep Into the Visuals

As you build these boards, think about style as substance. If you are a designer who loves grit and texture, your moodboard should reflect that. If you are focused on clean, corporate tech-motion, show that here. Your storyboards aren't just a "plan" -- they are the first time we see the world you are building.

Don't just draw what's happening. Draw HOW it's happening.
