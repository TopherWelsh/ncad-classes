    <div style="display: flex; flex-wrap: wrap; gap: 20px; margin-bottom: 30px;">
        <div style="flex: 1; min-width: 300px; padding: 20px; background: #f1f3f5; border-radius: 10px; border-top: 4px solid #343a40;">
            <h3 style="margin-top: 0; color: #212529;">📐 The Technical Pillars</h3>
            <ul style="padding-left: 20px;">
                <li><strong>3D Space:</strong> Use 3D layers and Z-depth to "fly" through the monologue.</li>
                <li><strong>Constant Motion:</strong> No dead frames. The camera or the type must always be evolving.</li>
                <li><strong>Reuse & Replace:</strong> Use letters from the previous word to form the next.</li>
                <li><strong>Motion Blur:</strong> Essential for high-speed Kinetic Type.</li>
            </ul>
        </div>
        <div style="flex: 1; min-width: 300px; padding: 20px; background: #f1f3f5; border-radius: 10px; border-top: 4px solid #0076c0;">
            <h3 style="margin-top: 0; color: #212529;">🎨 Narrative Style</h3>
            <ul style="padding-left: 20px;">
                <li><strong>Symbology:</strong> Represent nouns with typographic shapes.</li>
                <li><strong>Voice Hierarchy:</strong> Scale the type to match the volume/intensity of the speaker.</li>
                <li><strong>10 Principles:</strong> Apply eases, squash, and rhythm to every word.</li>
                <li><strong>Duration:</strong> Minimum of 30 seconds.</li>
            </ul>
