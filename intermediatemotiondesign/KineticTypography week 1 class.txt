    <h2 style="color: #888; font-size: 14px; text-transform: uppercase; border-bottom: 1px solid #ccc; padding-bottom: 5px; margin-top: 40px;">2. Core Theory: The Four Foundations</h2>
    <div style="display: flex; flex-wrap: wrap; gap: 20px; margin-top: 20px; margin-bottom: 40px;">
        <div style="flex: 1; min-width: 450px; background: #f8f9fa; padding: 25px; border-radius: 12px; border-left: 10px solid #1a1a1a;">
             <h3 style="margin-top: 0;">🛠️ Making it "Expensive" (Studio Tips)</h3>
             <p>Expensive edits aren't about doing <em>more</em>; they are about doing <em>less but better</em>. Avoid packing the screen with too many effects—this looks cheap.</p>
             <ul style="padding-left: 20px;">
                <li><strong>Font Psychology:</strong> The fastest way to look cheap is the wrong font. Use bold/heavy for strength and script for softness.</li>
                <li><strong>Cinematic Polish:</strong> Use adjustment layers for subtle film grain, halation, and S-curve color grading.</li>
                <li><strong>Intentional Sound:</strong> Every movement needs a sonic anchor. Add "clicks" for UI movements and "wooshes" for transitions.</li>
             </ul>
        </div>
        <div style="flex: 1; min-width: 400px; background: #1a1a1a; color: white; padding: 25px; border-radius: 12px;">
             <div style="position: relative; padding-bottom: 56.25%; height: 0; overflow: hidden; border-radius: 6px; margin-bottom: 15px;"><iframe style="position: absolute; top: 0; left: 0; width: 100%; height: 100%;" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/fAYVz0Oe3bE" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>
             <strong style="color: #00c6ff;">Key Nugget:</strong> "Movement should never be random. It must reflect the emotion or action described. Growing text evokes excitement; shrinking suggests diminishing importance."
        </div>
    </div>

    <h2 style="color: #888; font-size: 14px; text-transform: uppercase; border-bottom: 1px solid #ccc; padding-bottom: 5px; margin-top: 40px;">3. Technical Deep Dives (Workflows)</h2>
    <div style="display: flex; flex-wrap: wrap; gap: 20px; margin-top: 20px; margin-bottom: 40px;">
        
        <!-- Tutorial 1: Variable Fonts & Expressions -->
        <div style="flex: 1; min-width: 450px; background: #f1f3f5; padding: 25px; border-radius: 12px;">
            <div style="position: relative; padding-bottom: 56.25%; height: 0; overflow: hidden; border-radius: 6px; margin-bottom: 15px; background: #000;"><iframe style="position: absolute; top: 0; left: 0; width: 100%; height: 100%;" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/lzQwR5Gaz1A" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>
            <h4 style="margin-top: 0;">🧪 The Expression Workflow</h4>
            <p style="font-size: 0.9em;"><strong>The Nugget:</strong> Use After Effects expressions to cycle through <strong>variable font weights</strong>. By parenting a slider control to the "Source Text" of individual letters, you can create a "wave" effect where text morphs from Thin to Black in a single fluid motion.</p>
            <p style="font-size: 0.9em;"><strong>Depth Trick:</strong> Use <em>CC Spotlight</em> for a light/shadow falloff gradience, giving your text a 3D physical presence on the stage.</p>
        </div>

        <!-- Tutorial 2: Path Manipulation -->
        <div style="flex: 1; min-width: 450px; background: #f1f3f5; padding: 25px; border-radius: 12px;">
            <div style="position: relative; padding-bottom: 56.25%; height: 0; overflow: hidden; border-radius: 6px; margin-bottom: 15px; background: #000;"><iframe style="position: absolute; top: 0; left: 0; width: 100%; height: 100%;" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/h5F3QM72PY0" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>
            <h4 style="margin-top: 0;">📐 Per-Character 3D & Paths</h4>
            <p style="font-size: 0.9em;"><strong>The Nugget:</strong> Convert text to shapes (Right-click > Create Shapes from Text) to manipulate the actual paths. You can stretch points of a letter over time to create "Stretchy Type."</p>
            <p style="font-size: 0.9em;"><strong>The Rule:</strong> Strategic selection is key. Straight letters like "D" or "E" stretch beautifully; organic curves like "S" look messy when dragged. <em>Plan your words around your geometry.</em></p>
        </div>
    </div>

            <p style="margin: 0; color: #3e2723;">Use <strong>Track Mattes</strong> to carve out pieces of one layer to expose another. This is how you make text "snake" behind objects or appear as if it's being "sucked up" by a pipe. <em>Static masks + moving text = Professional depth.</em></p>
        </div>
        <div style="flex: 1; min-width: 450px; background-color: #e8f5e9; padding: 25px 25px 25px 60px; border: 2px solid #2e7d32; border-radius: 12px; position: relative;">

            <strong style="color: #1b5e20; text-transform: uppercase; font-size: 0.9em; display: block; margin-bottom: 5px;">Pro-Tip: Constant Motion</strong>
            <p style="margin: 0; color: #1b5e20;">Never let the eye rest. Use <strong>Global Scaling</strong> via a Null object. By parenting all layers to a single Null and animating a slow 100% to 120% scale, you create a "camera push" that keeps the composition dynamic even during static text moments.</p>

    <!-- ⚠️ PITFALL WARNING -->
    <div style="background-color: #ffebee; padding: 25px 25px 25px 60px; border: 2px solid #c62828; border-radius: 12px; margin-bottom: 40px; position: relative;">
        <span style="position: absolute; top: 25px; left: 20px; font-size: 1.8em;">⚠️</span>
        <strong style="color: #b71c1c; text-transform: uppercase; font-size: 0.9em; display: block; margin-bottom: 5px;">Common Pitfall: The OCD Trigger</strong>
        <p style="margin: 0; color: #4a0404;">If you don't center your type, you lose control of your rotations. Always <strong>Center Anchor Points</strong> in layer content (Ctrl+Alt+Home) before animating. If your rotations are wobbly, it’s usually because your anchor point is sitting in the corner of your bounding box.</p>
