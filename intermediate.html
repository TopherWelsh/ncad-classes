🎭 Closing "The Gap" (Ira Glass Philosophy)

Most beginners get into motion design because they have Good Taste. But for the first few years, your work doesn't match your taste. The only way to close the gap is to do a volume of work. Put yourself on a deadline so that every week you finish one project.
