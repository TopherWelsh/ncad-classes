How to Unlock Full Access (Upload Permissions)

Getting Write Access

By default, you have Read-Only access (downloads only). To upload large files or submit work that missed a Canvas lock, you need Write Access.

  • 1. You need a Google Email. If you don't have one, I highly recommend creating one for this course. It is the only way to get upload permissions.
  • 2. Give me your address. I will circulate a sign-up sheet in class. If you miss class, send it to me via Discord.

What's Inside the Drive?

Feel free to raid these folders for your personal projects, capstones, or experiments.

After Effects

  • Project Files: Deconstruct pro files to see how they organize layers.
  • Scripts & Plugins: A massive collection of utility scripts.
  • Presets: Drag-and-drop animations.

3D & Unreal

  • 3D Models: Characters, vehicles, and animals.
  • Blender Addons: Power tools for Blender.
  • Element 3D: Optimized textures/models.
  • VDBs: Volumetric clouds and smoke.

General Assets

  • Stock Footage: Clips for compositing.
  • Textures: Grunges, metals, papers.
  • Sound Design: Whooshes, hits, ambience.
  • Books: PDFs on design theory.
