Kinetic Typography

Mastering the art of "Moving Language." Learn how to transform static text into a living, breathing performance.

🎭 Closing "The Gap" (Ira Glass Philosophy)

Most beginners get into motion design because they have Good Taste. But for the first few years, your work doesn't match your taste. The only way to close the gap is to do a volume of work. Put yourself on a deadline so that every week you finish one project.

The Assignment

Select a monologue, joke, standup bit, or song lyric with a minimum duration of 30 seconds. Your goal is to build a complex, fast-paced Kinetic Typography piece that captures the essence of the audio.

Don't just make words appear. Use 3D space, symbology, and constant motion to tell the story. If the speaker mentions a "lock," don't just write the word -- make the letters "O" and "C" look like a mechanism snapping shut.

Professional Examples (The Benchmark)

Core Theory: The Four Foundations

Making it "Expensive" (Studio Tips)

Expensive edits aren't about doing more; they are about doing less but better.

  • Font Psychology: The fastest way to look cheap is the wrong font. Use bold/heavy for strength and script for softness.
  • Cinematic Polish: Use adjustment layers for subtle film grain, halation, and S-curve color grading.
  • Intentional Sound: Every movement needs a sonic anchor. Add "clicks" for UI movements and "wooshes" for transitions.

Key Nugget: Movement should never be random. It must reflect the emotion or action described.

Technical Pillars

3D & Motion

  • 3D Space: Use 3D layers and Z-depth to "fly" through the monologue.
  • Constant Motion: No dead frames. The camera or the type must always be evolving.
  • Reuse & Replace: Use letters from the previous word to form the next.
  • Motion Blur: Essential for high-speed Kinetic Type.

Narrative Style

  • Symbology: Represent nouns with typographic shapes.
  • Voice Hierarchy: Scale the type to match the volume/intensity of the speaker.
  • 10 Principles: Apply eases, squash, and rhythm to every word.
  • Duration: Minimum of 30 seconds.

Technical Deep Dives

The Expression Workflow

Use After Effects expressions to cycle through variable font weights. By parenting a slider control to the "Source Text" of individual letters, you can create a "wave" effect where text morphs from Thin to Black in a single fluid motion.

Depth Trick: Use CC Spotlight for a light/shadow falloff gradience, giving your text a 3D physical presence.

Per-Character 3D & Paths

Convert text to shapes (Right-click > Create Shapes from Text) to manipulate the actual paths. You can stretch points of a letter over time to create "Stretchy Type."

The Rule: Straight letters like "D" or "E" stretch beautifully; organic curves like "S" look messy when dragged. Plan your words around your geometry.

💡 Pro-Tip: Track Mattes & Masks

Use Track Mattes to carve out pieces of one layer to expose another. This is how you make text "snake" behind objects or appear as if it's being "sucked up" by a pipe.

🛠️ Pro-Tip: Constant Motion

Never let the eye rest. Use Global Scaling via a Null object. By parenting all layers to a single Null and animating a slow 100% to 120% scale, you create a "camera push" that keeps the composition dynamic.

💡 Pro-Tip: Dive Deep

Instead of moving the text to the camera, move the Camera through the text. Using After Effects cameras allows you to create incredible parallax and depth that makes a 2D exercise feel like a blockbuster film.

⚠️ Common Pitfall: The Subtitle Trap

Kinetic Typography is not just closed captioning. If the audience can read the words without seeing the animation, you haven't designed the type -- you've just transcribed it.

⚠️ Common Pitfall: Anchor Points

If you don't center your type, you lose control of your rotations. Always Center Anchor Points in layer content (Ctrl+Alt+Home) before animating. If your rotations are wobbly, it's usually because your anchor point is sitting in the corner of your bounding box.

Mastery Cheat Sheet

  • The "Gap": Closed only through volume of work.
  • Expensive Look: Less is more. Clean fonts + Motion.
  • Graph Editor: Snappy "in" and "out" curves.
  • 3D Focal Point: Blur the background to focus the eye.
  • CC Flow Motion: For that "warpy" high-end feel.
  • Sound Design: Clicks, switches, and whooshes.
