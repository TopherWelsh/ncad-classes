Week 9 • July 2, 2026

Boards, Styleframes, Motion Tests

Finish the storyboards, prove the look, test the motion language, and build an animatic before full production begins.

Discord Google Drive Signature Brief

The Goal

This week turns the idea into a production blueprint. Your storyboards should be complete, your visual direction should be proven through styleframes, and your timing should be tested in an animatic.

Lane-Specific Targets

Lane 1: Brand Motion System

  • Storyboards: Show the brand reveal, value/message, key product or identity beats, and end card.
  • Styleframes: Prove logo/type treatment, color system, campaign look, and layout rules.
  • Motion test: Test the logo reveal, type animation, product transition, or graphic system behavior.
  • Animatic: Confirm the spot feels like a real brand piece, not a slideshow.

Lane 2: Story / Explainer Film

  • Storyboards: Show the full explanation arc: hook, setup, key points, and takeaway.
  • Styleframes: Prove the visual metaphor system, type/caption style, and information hierarchy.
  • Motion test: Test a diagram, transition, character/object behavior, or complex explanation moment.
  • Animatic: Confirm the story is understandable before final animation begins.

Lane 3: Culture / Entertainment Package

  • Storyboards: Show the major hype beats, mood shifts, title moments, and final payoff.
  • Styleframes: Prove the visual world, key art direction, typography, and tone.
  • Motion test: Test a title reveal, beat-synced transition, camera move, or high-impact visual effect.
  • Animatic: Confirm the edit has rhythm and energy, not just cool frames.

Part 1: Finished Storyboards

  • Show every major beat from beginning to end.
  • Include camera notes, type placement, transitions, and sound notes.
  • Make the structure clear enough that another designer could understand the plan.

Part 2: Styleframes

  • Create high-fidelity frames that prove the final visual direction.
  • Show typography, color, composition, texture, and graphic language.
  • Make sure the frames feel like one consistent system.

Part 3: Motion Tests

  • Test the hardest motion problem before production week.
  • Explore transitions, type behavior, character/shape movement, camera moves, or visual effects.
  • Keep tests short, focused, and useful.

Part 4: Animatic

  • Put the boards into a timeline with rough timing.
  • Add scratch VO, temp music, or sound cues if needed.
  • Use the animatic to find pacing problems before animation begins.
💡 Production Shortcut

If the animatic feels boring, the final animation will probably feel boring too. Fix pacing now while the project is still cheap to change.

Week 9 Checklist

  • Storyboards complete: Full beginning, middle, and ending.
  • Styleframes created: Final look is visible.
  • Motion test exported: A focused test proves the motion language.
  • Animatic assembled: Boards are timed in sequence.
  • Audio considered: Music, VO, or sound direction is planned.
  • Production plan clear: You know what to animate next week.
