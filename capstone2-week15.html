The Finish Line

This submission should feel like a professional portfolio package. You are not only turning in an animation; you are showing the concept, process, design system, final deliverables, and your ability to manage a long-form project.

Lane-Specific Final Standard

Lane 1: Brand Motion System

  • Main brand spot feels client-ready.
  • Logo, type, color, and motion system are consistent.
  • Supporting assets look like a real campaign package.

Lane 2: Story / Explainer Film

  • Main film communicates the idea clearly.
  • Story, pacing, captions, and visual metaphors support comprehension.
  • Supporting assets extend or promote the explanation.

Lane 3: Culture / Entertainment Package

  • Main piece has a memorable hook, rhythm, and payoff.
  • Visual world feels distinct and portfolio-worthy.
  • Supporting assets feel ready for a launch, event, show, artist, or campaign.

Final Deliverables Checklist

Part 1: Main Motion Piece

  • Final .MP4 or .MOV render.
  • Clean audio mix, captions/type, and final polish.
  • Correct resolution and export settings.
  • Watch the final export before submitting.

Part 2: Supporting Assets

  • 3-5 supporting motion assets, cutdowns, title cards, posters, thumbnails, or system pieces.
  • Assets should feel connected to the main piece.
  • Export each asset separately and label clearly.

Part 3: Process + Case Study PDF

  • Project brief, audience, and lane.
  • Research, moodboards, styleframes, boards, motion tests, and animatic.
  • Final deliverables shown together.
  • Short reflection on what changed and what you learned.

Part 4: Collected Project Folder

  • Use After Effects Collect Files when possible.
  • Include assets, fonts, audio, renders, and source files.
  • Remove unused junk and clearly name folders.
  • Zip the complete root folder.
⚠️ Submission Pitfall

Do not upload only the .AEP file. If your assets are missing, the project is incomplete. Submit the final render separately from the zipped source package.
