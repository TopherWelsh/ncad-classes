Capstone 2 Extra Credit • 5 Points

Typefaces of Tacoma

Create a short-form typography documentary inspired by Typefaces of Toronto. Your subject is Tacoma: signs, storefronts, murals, plaques, windows, ghost signs, wayfinding, stickers, menus, or any public-facing local type.

The Challenge

Find one interesting piece of typography in Tacoma and turn it into a tight vertical short. The point is not just to show a sign. The point is to notice design in the real world and use motion, framing, pacing, and narration/text to make other people notice it too.

🔍 Reference Direction

Look up Typefaces of Toronto on Instagram and watch several of their reels/shorts before planning yours. Study the format: close observation, local typography, concise context, clean cuts, and a strong sense of place.

Required Outcome

Final Short

  • Length: 20-60 seconds.
  • Format: 1080x1920 vertical.
  • Subject: One specific Tacoma type specimen or small connected set.
  • Audio: Voiceover, captions, music, natural sound, or a combination.

Design Observation

  • Location: Tell us where the type was found.
  • Visual traits: Describe shape, era, material, color, spacing, texture, or style.
  • Context: Explain what the type says about the business, street, neighborhood, or culture.
  • Motion treatment: Use movement to guide attention, not just decorate the edit.

Shot List Starter

Establishing Shot

Show the street, building, storefront, alley, or object so the type has a place.

Clean Type Close-Up

Capture the letterforms clearly. Avoid shaky unreadable footage.

Texture Detail

Show paint, neon, vinyl, rust, glass, metal, brick, weathering, or hand-made quirks.

Motion Reveal

Use a pan, push-in, mask, wipe, match cut, or kinetic caption to reveal the most interesting detail.

Caption Moment

Add one concise fact, observation, or question that makes viewers look closer.

Final Beauty Shot

End with the type looking intentional, cinematic, and memorable.

Possible Tacoma Targets

Old Signage

Ghost signs, neon, painted storefronts, vintage logos, old theaters, diners, repair shops, warehouses.

Street Culture

Murals, stickers, venue posters, hand-painted windows, record shops, coffee shops, skate or music spaces.

Institutional Type

Museum labels, wayfinding, transit signs, campus lettering, plaques, civic buildings, historic markers.

5 Point Scoring Guide

What Earns the Points

  • 1 point: Clear Tacoma typography subject.
  • 1 point: Strong footage or imagery with readable type.
  • 1 point: Thoughtful design observation, not just a location tour.
  • 1 point: Motion/editing supports the story.
  • 1 point: Polished vertical export with clean sound/captions.
