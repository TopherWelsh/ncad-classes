Production Week

This is a heads-down production week. Your animatic and hero frames are locked. Now it's time to bring the first five principles to life through final keyframe animation.

Scenes to Animate This Week

Scene 1: Timing & Spacing

The "beat" of your film. Establish the rhythm that drives every subsequent principle. Use the Value Graph to show the difference between uniform and dynamic spacing.

Scene 2: Eases

Demonstrate the difference between linear, ease-in, ease-out, and custom bezier curves. The Value Graph is your primary tool here.

Scene 3: Mass & Weight

Make the audience feel the weight. Heavy objects accelerate slowly and impact hard. Light objects float and bounce.

Scene 4: Anticipation

The wind-up before the action. Every major movement needs a preparatory motion in the opposite direction.

Scene 5: Arcs

Natural motion follows curved paths. Nothing in nature moves in perfectly straight lines. Use motion paths to prove it.

💡 Pro-Tip: The Value Graph

Don't just set keyframes and hope for the best. Open the Graph Editor and sculpt every curve. The Value Graph is where amateur animation becomes professional motion. Spend 80% of your time here.

✨ Appeal Check

Even in the physics-heavy scenes, your Hero must maintain its personality. If the character feels "robotic" while demonstrating Timing and Spacing, you're missing the Appeal layer. Every scene should feel like a performance, not a tutorial.
