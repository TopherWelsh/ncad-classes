📣 Status: Final Project Management Check

This is the last major accountability checkpoint before final polish. The goal is to catch the gaps while you can still make smart production decisions.

Lane-Specific Final Check

Lane 1: Brand Motion System

  • Does the main spot communicate the brand clearly?
  • Are the logo, typography, color, and motion rules consistent?
  • Do the supporting assets expand the system instead of feeling random?

Lane 2: Story / Explainer Film

  • Is the explanation clear from start to finish?
  • Are any sections overloaded with too much information?
  • Does the final takeaway land clearly?

Lane 3: Culture / Entertainment Package

  • Does the piece feel exciting enough for its subject?
  • Are the strongest moments placed where the audience will remember them?
  • Do the supporting assets feel campaign-ready?

What to Submit

  • Full rough cut or near-final render.
  • Current supporting assets.
  • Current process/case study document.
  • Collected project folder or organized working folder.

What We Are Checking

  • Does the project communicate clearly?
  • Does the visual system feel consistent?
  • Is the animation strong enough for portfolio use?
  • What must be cut, simplified, or polished?
⚠️ No Heroic Last-Minute Rebuilds

If something is not working by now, the professional move may be to simplify it. A finished, focused project beats an overbuilt project that collapses in the final week.
