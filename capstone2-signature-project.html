Capstone 2 • Weeks 8-15 • Graduating Signature Project

Motion With a Purpose

Choose one professional lane, build one complete motion design project, and prove you can take an idea from concept to polished delivery.

Discord Google Drive Storyboard Template

The Challenge

Create a polished motion design project that could live in your graduating portfolio and clearly communicates an idea, identity, story, or experience. This project should feel like a professional case study, not just a class exercise.

Choose One Project Lane

Lane 1: Brand Motion System

Create a motion package for a brand, event, product, artist, nonprofit, app, company, or fictional identity.

  • 20-45 second launch spot or brand anthem.
  • Animated logo or identity sting.
  • 3-5 supporting social assets or motion moments.
  • Strong typography, visual system, and consistency.

Lane 2: Story / Explainer Film

Make a short animated piece that explains, reveals, or reframes an idea for a specific audience.

  • 45-90 second animated explainer or mini-documentary.
  • Script, voiceover, captions, or text-driven structure.
  • Clear visual metaphor system.
  • Designed to make something easier to understand.

Lane 3: Culture / Entertainment Package

Build a motion piece around music, film, games, sports, fashion, events, streaming, or an imagined media property.

  • 30-60 second title sequence, trailer, opener, or promo.
  • Poster, thumbnail, key art, or promo system.
  • Sound/music-driven edit.
  • Bold visual direction with a memorable hook.
🧠 Freedom Inside a Container

You have creative freedom, but not unlimited wandering room. You must choose a lane, define a subject, identify an audience, and build a complete project with a clear purpose.

Required Final Package

Main Motion Piece

  • Brand Lane: 20-45 second launch spot or brand film.
  • Story Lane: 45-90 second explainer or narrative film.
  • Culture Lane: 30-60 second opener, title sequence, trailer, or event promo.

Supporting Assets

  • 3-5 cutdowns, social assets, title cards, or motion system pieces.
  • Process PDF with research, moodboards, styleframes, boards, tests, and iteration.
  • Final portfolio case study presentation.

Project Timeline

Weeks 8-15 Roadmap

  • Week 8: Lane, subject, audience, brief, research, moodboards, concept, script outline, storyboards assigned.
  • Week 9: Storyboards complete, styleframes, motion tests, and animatic.
  • Week 10: Animatic to animation production.
  • Week 11: Mid-project check-in.
  • Week 12: Production sprint and supporting assets.
  • Week 13: Home stretch check-in.
  • Week 14: Final polish and portfolio case study.
  • Week 15: Final submission and presentation.
