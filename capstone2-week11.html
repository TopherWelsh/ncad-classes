Mid-Project Check-In

Turn in the current state of the Signature Project so we can catch problems while there is still time to fix them.

Discord Google Drive Signature Brief
📣 Status: Accountability Check

This is a project management checkpoint. You are not being judged for perfection; you are being held accountable to the production timeline.

Lane-Specific Review Questions

Lane 1: Brand Motion System

  • Does the brand feel clear and consistent?
  • Is the logo/type motion strong enough to be portfolio-facing?
  • Do the supporting assets feel like the same campaign?

Lane 2: Story / Explainer Film

  • Can the viewer understand the idea without extra explanation?
  • Are the visual metaphors helping or confusing the message?
  • Is the pacing too slow, too dense, or just right?

Lane 3: Culture / Entertainment Package

  • Does the piece have a strong hook and payoff?
  • Does the rhythm match the music, subject, or event energy?
  • Is the style memorable enough to stand out in a portfolio?

What to Submit

  • Current .AEP project file and organized assets.
  • Current progress render as .MP4 or .MOV.
  • Updated boards or styleframes if your direction changed.
  • Any current supporting assets or cutdowns.

What to Ask For

  • Where is the pacing weak?
  • Which sections feel under-designed?
  • What animation problem is slowing you down?
  • What needs to be simplified to hit the deadline?
⚠️ Hard Deadline

This check-in is designed to protect your final grade and your final piece. Submit the project as it exists, even if it feels rough.
