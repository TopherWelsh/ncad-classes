🔥 The Mid-Term Push

This is a multi-week sprint leading toward your midterm. While it is super easy to go "heads down" and hide in your own little world -- especially with a project this personal -- I am encouraging you to stay connected.

The Discord Community

Post your WIPs (Works In Progress) in the Discord frequently. Why?

  • The Alumni & Juniors: They are watching and learning from you.
  • Peer Perspectives: There is creativity in everyone's eyes. Even if you don't take every piece of advice, it's invaluable to hear how someone else sees your vision.
  • Constructive Growth: Asking for opinions while you are learning is the fastest way to improve.

Stuck? Don't Bang Your Head

Motion design is hard. If you are stuck on a technical hurdle or a weird expression error, don't waste hours frustrated.

Post it in Discord! It is the fastest place to troubleshoot. Someone else has likely already solved the exact problem you are facing.

📣 The 15-Minute Rule

If you can't figure it out in 15 minutes, it's time to ask for help. Don't waste hours frustrated when a quick Discord message can unblock you.

Topher's Availability
