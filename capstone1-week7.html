💼 The Presentation Mindset

In this industry, being an amazing artist is only half the battle. The other half is the ability to clearly, confidently, and professionally build and present your ideas from scratch. From this point forward, every Capstone project will be held to this standard.

The 9 Deliverables

Motion Manifesto

Your narrative reflection of your "Why." (Can be public or private).

Mood Boards

Visual inspiration with a written explanation of the style's meaning.

Professional Script

A properly formatted script detailing VO and on-screen text.

Clean Storyboards

Professional layouts with digital text only. No messy handwriting.

Style Frames

The "Hero Shots" that act as the final visual benchmark.

The Animatic

The timed-out blueprint of your film, synced to your audio track.

Process Breakdowns

(Optional) Show off technical hurdles you conquered along the way.

FINAL PRODUCT

Your completed 60-90 second masterpiece in its final render.

The Case Study

Published live on your website, Behance, LinkedIn, or Portfolio.
