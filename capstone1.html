✨ Finding Your "Why" Again

Over the last year, you've been through the grind -- Intro, Intermediate, and Advanced Motion Design. Somewhere in that technical noise, it's easy to lose sight of why you started this journey. This project is about finding your way back to YOU. Don't design what you think I want to see. Design what you've been waiting to create all year.

Project Specs

The Motion Manifesto

  • Duration: 60 - 90 Seconds
  • Aspect Ratios: 16:9, 1:1, or 9:16
  • Software: Student Choice (AE, Blender, Unreal, etc.)
  • Technique: Multi-Technique Stack (Show your range!)
  • Audio: Music, SFX, and optional Voiceover

Privacy & Sharing

This project is deeply personal. Two tiers of sharing:

  • Private (Prof Toph Only): Your Written Manifesto. This is your internal discovery phase.
  • Public (The Class): Moodboards, Storyboards, Animatics, and Final Production.
