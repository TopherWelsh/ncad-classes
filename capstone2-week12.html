Week 12 • July 23, 2026

Production Sprint + Supporting Assets

Keep building the main piece while turning the project into a complete portfolio package.

Discord Google Drive Signature Brief

The Goal

This week is about momentum. Your main piece should be moving toward a full rough cut, and your supporting assets should start becoming real deliverables instead of a note on the assignment sheet.

Lane-Specific Supporting Assets

Lane 1: Brand Motion System

  • Animated logo sting or end tag.
  • Lower third, title card, transition, or graphic overlay.
  • Social cutdown or product/brand teaser.
  • Make every asset feel like it came from the same brand guide.

Lane 2: Story / Explainer Film

  • Short teaser clip using the hook or strongest explanation moment.
  • Diagram, title card, chapter card, or caption system.
  • Poster/key art that summarizes the idea visually.
  • Make the support pieces help the audience understand the topic.

Lane 3: Culture / Entertainment Package

  • Poster, thumbnail, or key art.
  • Social promo, countdown, bumper, or hype loop.
  • Title card, lower third, transition, or event graphic.
  • Make the assets feel like a complete entertainment campaign.

Main Piece Progress

  • Animate remaining major sections.
  • Refine rough sections after feedback.
  • Start smoothing transitions and pacing.
  • Export progress renders often.

Supporting Asset Progress

  • Create 3-5 cutdowns, social assets, stings, posters, title cards, or motion system pieces.
  • Keep them visually connected to the main piece.
  • Make them feel useful in a real campaign or portfolio case study.
💡 Portfolio Thinking

The supporting assets are not busywork. They make the project look like a real professional package instead of one isolated animation.

Week 12 Checklist

  • Rough cut advancing: Most major sections exist.
  • Feedback applied: Week 11 notes are being addressed.
  • Transitions started: The piece is beginning to connect.
  • Supporting assets started: 3-5 extras are in progress.
  • Export reviewed: You have watched the full piece outside AE.
  • Next questions clear: You know what needs review in Week 13.
