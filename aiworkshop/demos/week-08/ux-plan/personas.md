# Plant App Personas - Hypotheses Only

## Persona A: Apartment Beginner
**UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS:** A first-time plant owner in a small apartment wants simple diagnosis without botany vocabulary.

Test questions:
1. What words do they use for plant problems?
2. Do they trust photo diagnosis?
3. What step feels doable on a weekday?
4. What would make them stop using the app?
5. Do reminders help or annoy them?

## Persona B: Overloaded Collector
**UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS:** A hobbyist with many plants needs triage, not generic care tips.

Test questions:
1. How many plants before tracking becomes useful?
2. What symptoms trigger urgency?
3. Do they want confidence levels?
4. How do they currently document care?
5. What would they never trust an app to decide?
