Part 2

Future-proofing

~3:05. The evaluation system.

New tools arrive constantly

The skill is evaluating them fast and calmly

Not fear-adopting. Not fear-refusing.

"You've used pieces of this pattern all term. Now it's one move."

5 minutes, any new tool

The evaluation pattern

  1. What friction does it actually remove?
  2. Training-data / rights situation? (Week 4)
  3. Control, continuity, cost? (Week 5)
  4. What does it touch / where's my data? (Weeks 11–12)
  5. My friction or my art? (Week 14)
  6. What would I disclose if I used it?
"Run a new tool through these and you'll decide better than 90% of the internet."

The hype filter

Every tool is announced as revolutionary

Wait two weeks. Read a skeptic. Try one real task. Check what it actually removed.

Most tools are a feature, not a future.

"The few that matter will still matter in a month."
