The form rules

BBC guideline essentials

  • ≤ 42 characters per line · 2 lines max
  • Break at sense boundaries — never mid-name
  • Keep the speaker's rhythm — don't formalize the voice
  • Sound information when it matters: [door slams]

Part 2

The multimodal critique relay

Works on everything you make

~1:00. The generalizable half of the week.

The capability

Models can see now

Screenshot anything — frame, layout, model sheet, UI — and run structured critique against your criteria.

Filtered by you: a second pair of eyes. Unfiltered: noise.

The filtering is the skill.

Callback to Week 2's critique loop — this is its visual sibling.

The method

The relay, five steps

  1. Screenshot the WIP
  2. Three named reviewers — 3 observations + 1 priority fix each
  3. You filter — accept/reject with written reasons
  4. Peer challenges one accept + one reject
  5. Triage: blocker / should-fix / polish + minutes
Step 3 carries the week: "At least one critique will be confidently wrong. Rejecting it well is the most professional move you'll make today."

The artifact

The triage list is the deliverable

Blocker / should-fix / polish — with minutes per fix.

It shows you can convert feedback into a plan.
That's a producer's skill, and it's hireable.

"Your next work session, pre-planned."

And sound

Sound briefs + the music wall

Describing sound is a deliverable: "dry clicks, room tone, one swell."

Generated music stays out of deliverables — rights contested, active litigation. Scratch, private, done.

Tools doc has the current state.
