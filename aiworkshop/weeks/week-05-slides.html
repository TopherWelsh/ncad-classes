Part 1, continued

The treatment

120 words that decide everything

~0:20.

Treatment anatomy

The five slots

  1. Visual approach — territory language (Week 3 vocabulary)
  2. Pacing logic — builds, holds, cuts, breathes
  3. Type behaviour — type is a character; say how it acts
  4. Palette — named, limited
  5. Sound feeling — even one line changes everything
"Type behaviour is the slot students forget. Does it assemble? Resist? Breathe?"

Compare

Weak vs. five-slot

Vibes

"Energetic and modern with bold typography"

Decisions

"Type assembles from debris on each beat; holds 2s on the verb; palette rust/bone; sound: dry clicks, one swell"

Left one is real (students write it constantly). Right one can be animated tomorrow morning.

The move

The edit-two-lines move

AI drafts the treatment.

You rewrite the two lines that matter.

After that, it's yours — and you'll feel the difference.

This move appears in the demo. It's also the integrity answer for treatments.

The shot list

Shot # · duration · action · camera · type on screen · asset needed · transition out

Prime AI territory. Audited by you.

Show the prepared example briefly.

Reliable failure #1

The maths

You asked for 15 seconds.
It produced 17, in a confident table.

It does arithmetic-shaped output.
You do arithmetic.

The calculator on the desk is a bit, but it's also not.

Reliable failure #2

Fantasy shots

It will cheerfully specify the fluid sim you cannot make by Friday.

Killing those shots isn't failure. It's called producing.

"Every kill gets a replacement spec'd to your real toolkit."

Memorize this

The 60-second timing audit

  1. Sum the durations column — by hand
  2. First 2 seconds: does something earn attention?
  3. Breath: one hold ≥ 1.5s, or all churn?
  4. The out: resolve, or just stop?
This audit runs in the lab on every pack.

Lab · 70 minutes

One Prompt, Three Treatments

3 treatments (15) → edit yours (10) → shot list + audit (15) → style frame (15) → tasks (10) → pack (5)

Standing message: "Slow down — Dublin moves better when it breathes"

Parts + times on board.
Lab · step 1

15 min

Three contrasting treatments

  1. Kinetic typography — type is the only performer
  2. Product/object spot — one hero object, real light
  3. Abstract title sequence — texture carries the feeling

Pick one + write two sentences on WHY.

The pick rationale is assessed thinking. Circulate: "What does your pick say about your taste?"
Lab · steps 2–3

25 min

Make it yours, then audit it

Edit: rewrite the two most load-bearing lines in your own words.

Shot list: build it → sum durations BY HAND → kill fantasy shots.

Spot-check three students' sums personally. Celebrate caught errors loudly.
Lab · steps 4–6

30 min

Frame, tasks, pack

Frame: generate (Week 4 discipline) — or full frame brief + thumbnail sketch.

Tasks: generate → producer's edit.

Pack: treatment + shots + frame + tasks.

Zero-gen frame brief = the professional document version. Say so.
Always available

Zero-generation track

The entire pack except the frame image is text.

The frame becomes prompt + sketch —
studios literally call this a frame brief.

Equal credit, professional document.
