Part 1, continued

Positioning

The sentence everything hangs on

~0:18.

The formula

For [narrow audience], [name] is the [category] that [genuine difference].

Everything downstream argues for this sentence — or doesn't belong.

Type, palette, voice, layout: all evidence for the sentence.

The problem

AI positioning is soup

"For music lovers, Toy Cathedral is the band that brings energy."

Competent. Fluent. Describes ten thousand bands.

Read it flat. Let it die in the air.

The move of the week

The de-souper

Strike every word a competitor could also claim.

What survives is your difference.
If nothing survives — the brand doesn't have one yet.
That discovery is billable.

"Not a prompt problem. A strategy discovery." Runs live in the demo.

Part 1, continued

Systems, not assets

~0:26.

A brand is rules

The system's four parts

  • Voice: 4 adjectives, each with "this not that"
  • Type: 2 pairings with rationale tied to positioning
  • Palette: colours + where each is allowed
  • Layout logic: grid habits, spacing personality
"Swatches are decoration. Usage rules are the system."

Quality control

The rationale test

Reject any type rationale that could justify anything.

✗ "Clean and modern" — justifies everything
✓ "The slab's bluntness matches 'playful, not precious'" — a decision

Demand rationale cite the positioning. Recurs in the demo.

Copy

The tone spec

Your 4 adjectives + this-not-thats
+ a banned-words list:

elevate · seamless · vibrant · discover · [yours]

"Without a tone spec you get LinkedIn voice. A good banned-words list is a brand asset."
