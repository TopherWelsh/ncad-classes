Part 1, the core

The extraction interview

~0:26.

Don't ask AI to write it

Make it interview you

  • The constraint that hurt
  • The decision you'd defend in a fight
  • The thing you cut that you loved
  • What the client actually said
  • What you'd do differently
Same interview move as Weeks 6 and 11. Its questions, your answers, your authorship.

Then

"Write using ONLY my answers,
my phrasing where possible,
no adjectives I didn't earn."

The constraint that makes it yours instead of generic.

Part 1, continued

The Generic Detector

~0:32.

AI-voice has tells

Every recruiter recognizes them

passionate about · seamless · elevate ·
stunning visuals · user-centric · I'm excited to

…because every AI writes them.

Board these. Students find them in their own drafts and gasp.

Watch out

AI also INFLATES

Results you didn't measure. A role bigger than you had. Impact you can't prove.

Deflate it.

"Recruiters check. Getting caught inflating ends candidacies on the spot."
Differentiator

Disclosure

Specific disclosure reads as confidence

Worst assumption

"AI-assisted"

Trust

"Textures generated, then repainted by hand; concept and art direction mine"

"It shows you know exactly where your value sits."

The extraction interview

Don't write anything yet. Interview me, one question at a time, hunting for the constraint that hurt, the decision I'd defend, the thing I cut.

The honest rewrite

Lab · 70 minutes

Generic Detector + Case Study Rewrite

Generate + detect (15) → Extraction interview (25) → Deflate + detect (15) → Prompt pack (15)

Bring a real project — or your strongest coursework.

Parts + times on board.
Lab · step 1

15 min

Generate + detect

Generate the generic case study of YOUR project. Run the Generic Detector. Highlight every stock phrase.

Seeing AI write your generic version is the wake-up call.

Let it land before moving on.
Lab · step 2

25 min

Extraction interview

Run the interview. Answer with real specifics and stakes. Get the rewrite.

The assessed deliverable — it must read like YOU.

Circulate: "What's the detail here that's only true of YOUR project?" None? Send them back.
Lab · step 3

15 min

Deflate + detect again

Challenge every result/role/impact claim. Replace surviving stock phrases by hand.

✏️ the lines only you could have written.

"Did you measure that 30%? No? Then it's gone or it's honest."
Lab · step 4

15 min

Prompt pack

Customize at least three job-search prompts into your reusable pack.

Core: role matcher · interview rehearsal · outreach structurer.

The take-home career tool. Reusable for years.
