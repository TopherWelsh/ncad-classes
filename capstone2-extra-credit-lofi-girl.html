Lo-Fi Girl Styled Video

Create a cozy animated loop inspired by lo-fi study videos, bedroom/loft ambience, and small environmental details that make a scene feel alive.

Discord Google Drive Back to Capstone 2

The Challenge

Design a short animated ambience piece built around one character, one room, and one mood. The loop should feel calm, intentional, and watchable without needing a plot.

🏡 Style Translation

This can be a classic lo-fi study girl setup, a cozy desk scene, a rainy window scene, a late-night studio scene, or your own version of that visual language. Avoid copying an existing character exactly. Build your own scene.

Inspiration: Lo-Fi Study Worlds

Lofi Girl: The core visual reference for character, desk, window, and micro-motion ambience.

Synthwave / Study Room: Similar format with a different lighting mood and environmental language.

Loop Study Reference: Watch for repeatable cycles, subtle movement, and calm pacing.

Animated Ambience: Look at how the scene uses small details instead of big action.

Helpful Tutorials

Character Loop: A practical look at turning yourself into a lo-fi girl style animation.

Rain / Window Motion: Useful for adding atmosphere to a cozy room loop.

Loop Reference: Study the timing and repeatability of a simple lo-fi animation loop.

Scene Motion: Reference for keeping a quiet animated scene visually alive.

Required Outcome

Final Loop

  • Length: 10-30 seconds.
  • Format: 1920x1080 horizontal or 1080x1920 vertical.
  • Loop: Beginning and ending should connect cleanly or feel intentionally repeatable.
  • Sound: Optional, but ambience or lo-fi music can strengthen the mood.

Scene Design

  • Character: One clear main figure, silhouette, or presence.
  • Environment: A room or space with enough detail to feel lived in.
  • Mood: A readable emotional tone: cozy, lonely, focused, rainy, dreamy, tired, warm, etc.
  • Motion: Multiple subtle animated details, not just one moving layer.

Motion Detail Menu

Character Loop

Blinking, breathing, hair movement, writing, typing, drinking, head nods, foot taps.

Window Life

Rain, drifting clouds, city lights, cars passing, moonlight, sunset shifts, curtains moving.

Desk Details

Screen glow, lamp flicker, steam from a mug, page flips, sticky notes, plants, headphones.

Atmosphere

Dust motes, film grain, soft light rays, shadows, drifting particles, subtle camera parallax.

Color Shift

Small lighting changes that create time of day, warmth, coolness, or emotional contrast.

Loop Trick

Use repeating cycles, hidden cuts, ping-pong motion, or match-frame transitions.

💡 Portfolio Upgrade

This is a strong place to practice art direction. A simple scene with good lighting, composition, and 6-10 tiny motion details will beat a complicated scene that feels unfinished.

5 Point Scoring Guide

What Earns the Points

  • 1 point: Clear scene, character, and mood.
  • 1 point: Strong composition and visual style.
  • 1 point: Multiple environmental motion details.
  • 1 point: Loop feels smooth or intentionally repeatable.
  • 1 point: Polished export with clean timing and no broken edges.
